About CareQueue

A digital appointment management platform built to make healthcare visits simpler for patients and doctors alike.

Our Purpose

Care That Stays Organized

CareQueue connects patients with the right specialists and removes the confusion of waiting rooms and paper slips. From choosing a department to receiving a digital prescription, every step happens in one place.

The platform serves three roles — patients, doctors, and administrators — each with a focused dashboard designed around the tasks they perform every day.
